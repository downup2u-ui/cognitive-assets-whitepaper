# Decision Log

This is a canonical MCAP file.

## Purpose

Use this file to preserve major decisions and the reasoning behind them, including context, options considered, chosen path, rejected paths, risks accepted, authority status, evidence references, and reopen conditions.

A decision without its reasoning becomes fragile. A future user may know what was chosen but not why the choice made sense.

## What Should Not Go Here

Do not include decisions attributed to AI as final authority, unreviewed AI recommendations presented as approved decisions, confidential deliberations that should be summarized or redacted, or decisions without owner, date, authority status, and review status when those fields are available.
