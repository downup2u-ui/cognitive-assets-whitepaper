## Handoff Rules

Before handing this work to another person or AI system, provide:

- current project context
- active decisions
- judgment rules
- known risks
- unresolved questions
- review status

## Stop Conditions

Stop or escalate when:

- context is missing
- authority boundaries are unclear
- the action is irreversible
- sensitive information may be exposed
- claims cannot be verified
- the workflow conflicts with judgment rules

## What Should Not Go Here

Do not include credentials, operational secrets, irreversible automation instructions without review gates, workflows that bypass human authority for consequential decisions, or outdated procedures unless marked as retired.
