## Source Boundaries

Do not treat the following as verified evidence without review:

- AI-generated citations
- unsourced summaries
- memory-based claims
- incomplete notes
- outdated references

## What Should Not Go Here

Do not include fabricated citations, unverifiable claims presented as verified evidence, copyrighted source material beyond permitted use, confidential sources that should be anonymized, or private communications without permission.
